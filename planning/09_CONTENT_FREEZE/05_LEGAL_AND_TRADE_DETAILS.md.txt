# Phase 9.5 — Content & Asset Freeze
## 05_LEGAL_AND_TRADE_DETAILS

This file defines how legal, trade, and regulatory information is presented on the website.

All references must remain general and non-committal unless verified.

---

## HSN Codes

HSN codes for products are not publicly displayed on the website.

Reason:
- Codes may vary by product grade, use, or jurisdiction
- Disclosure without confirmation may lead to errors

Allowed statement:
“HSN codes and trade classifications are available upon request.”

---

## Export & Trade Representation

Allowed:
- “Serving domestic and international markets”
- “Export-oriented supply”

Forbidden:
- Listing of specific countries
- Claims of export volume or reach
- Use of flags or country icons

---

## Regulatory Representation

The website must not:
- Claim regulatory approvals
- Imply government authorization
- Display statutory numbers or licenses

Allowed:
- “Regulatory documentation can be shared upon inquiry.”

---

## Disclaimers

Where applicable, include general disclaimers such as:
“Information provided on this website is indicative and subject to confirmation.”

---

## Change Control

If verified legal or trade documentation is provided:
- This file must be updated first
- Only then may details be added to pages or documents

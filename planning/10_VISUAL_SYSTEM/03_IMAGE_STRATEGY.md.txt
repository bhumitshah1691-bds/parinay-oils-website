# Phase 10 — Visual & UX System
## 03_IMAGE_STRATEGY

### Purpose of Images
- Reinforce credibility
- Show real operations and products
- Reduce abstraction where clarity helps

### Allowed Image Types
- Manufacturing facilities
- Product close-ups (real)
- Warehousing, logistics, export handling
- Process-oriented visuals

### Forbidden Image Types
- Generic stock models
- Fake laboratory imagery
- Over-stylized abstract graphics
- Lifestyle or aspirational imagery

### Placement Rules
- Images must support text, not replace it
- One image per major section maximum
- No image-only sections

### Quantity Control
- Home: limited, purposeful
- Product pages: minimal, factual
- Content pages: optional, not required

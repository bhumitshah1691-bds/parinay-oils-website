# Phase 12 — Visual Impact Layer
## 01_BRAND_COLOR_SYSTEM

### Objective
Create immediate visual authority for Indian buyers using logo-led color confidence, while preserving export-grade seriousness and long-term credibility.

Color must signal:
- Agro-industrial scale
- Commodity strength
- Stability and trust
- Established operations

### Color Source of Truth
The Parinay Oils logo is the sole color authority.

No colors may be introduced that are not visually compatible with the logo greens.

### Primary Brand Color
Deep Industrial Green (from logo)

Usage:
- Header accents
- Hero section emphasis
- Primary buttons
- Key separators and highlights

Rules:
- Must be visible above the fold
- Must repeat consistently across pages
- Never decorative
- Never used in gradients

### Secondary Brand Color
Dark Forest Green / Charcoal-Green

Usage:
- Headings
- Footer background
- Strong contrast sections

Purpose:
- Anchor seriousness
- Avoid “fresh FMCG” feel
- Maintain export-grade tone

### Background Neutrals
- Off-white / warm light grey for main sections
- Very light green-tinted neutral blocks for section framing

Rules:
- No pure white pages
- No harsh contrast
- Backgrounds must feel calm and agricultural, not clinical

### Accent Usage (Strict)
Allowed:
- Thin lines
- Dividers
- Hover states
- Underlines for emphasis

Forbidden:
- Bright accent colors
- Decorative shapes
- Visual noise
- Gradient effects

### Psychological Target
At first glance:
“Established agro company. Serious operator. Trustworthy supplier.”

### Guardrails
- No new colors without Phase 12 revision
- No gradients, glass effects, or glow
- Green must communicate authority, not softness

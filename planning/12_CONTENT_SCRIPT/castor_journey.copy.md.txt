Stage 01 — Flower

Castor cultivation begins with controlled flowering in suitable agro-climatic conditions. Crop quality is influenced at this earliest stage through seed selection and field practices.

Stage 02 — Seeds

Mature flowers develop into castor seed pods. Seed characteristics determine oil yield and downstream suitability for industrial processing.

Stage 03 — Harvesting

Castor seeds are harvested at optimal maturity to preserve oil integrity. Post-harvest handling minimizes contamination and moisture variation.

Stage 04 — Processing

Seeds undergo mechanical and controlled extraction processes. Filtration and refining are applied based on end-use requirements and specifications.

Stage 05 — Oil

Refined castor oil is prepared for industrial, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, and specialty applications, meeting defined quality and consistency parameters.

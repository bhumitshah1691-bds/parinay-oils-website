# Phase 9.5 — Content & Asset Freeze
## 04_CERTIFICATIONS_AND_COMPLIANCE

This file defines certification and compliance representation rules for the website.

No certification, approval, or compliance claim may be made unless explicitly listed here.

---

## Current Certification Status

At present, no certifications are publicly declared on the website.

This includes (but is not limited to):
- ISO
- GMP
- HACCP
- Organic
- Any government or export certifications

---

## Website Representation Rules

The website must:
- Avoid certification logos
- Avoid certification badges
- Avoid compliance icons
- Avoid statements implying certified operations

Allowed statements:
- “Quality and compliance details can be shared upon inquiry.”
- “Documentation available on request.”

---

## Visual Guardrails

Forbidden visuals:
- Certification seals
- Checkmarks implying approval
- Laboratory imagery implying certification
- Government emblems or flags

Illustrative visuals may be used only if they do not imply compliance status.

---

## Legal Safety Rule

No part of the website should imply that Parinay Oils holds certifications unless confirmed in writing by the company owner.

Misrepresentation risk is considered critical.

---

## Change Control

If certifications are later confirmed:
- This file must be updated first
- Only then may certification references be added to pages or documents

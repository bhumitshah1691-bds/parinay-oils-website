## Product Scope

Parinay Oils operates in two distinct capacities:

1. Manufactured Product
- Castor Oil

2. Traded / Sourced Product
- Castor Seed

Manufacturing activities are limited strictly to Castor Oil.
Castor Seed is supplied through sourcing and trading arrangements.

This separation must remain explicit across the site.

---

## Castor Oil (Manufactured)

### Product Description
Castor Oil is manufactured by Parinay Oils using controlled processing methods with a focus on consistency, purity, and buyer-specific requirements.

The product is supplied for industrial and commercial applications where castor oil is used as a base input or functional component.

### Positioning Rules
- Refer to Castor Oil as a manufactured product
- Do not imply downstream processing beyond oil extraction
- Do not imply retail or consumer-grade positioning

---

## Castor Seed (Traded / Sourced)

### Product Description
Castor Seed is supplied by Parinay Oils through external sourcing and trading arrangements aligned with quality and supply requirements.

The company does not process or manufacture castor seed products.

### Positioning Rules
- Clearly label Castor Seed as traded or sourced
- Do not imply manufacturing, processing, or value addition
- Do not imply storage or grading facilities unless confirmed

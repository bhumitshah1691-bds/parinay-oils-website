# Phase 12 — Visual Impact Layer
## 03_SECTION_FRAMING_SYSTEM

### Objective
Create visible visual weight and richness across pages so the website feels like a serious, established Indian agro company.

The goal is to avoid “document-like” pages and instead present structured, intentional sections.

---

### Core Principle
No content block should float freely.

Every major section must feel:
- Intentionally framed
- Visually separated
- Clearly grouped

Whitespace alone is not enough for Indian market perception.

---

### Section Background Strategy

Allowed:
- Alternating background blocks using:
  - Off-white
  - Light warm grey
  - Very light green-tinted neutral

Rules:
- No long stretches of pure white
- Background changes must feel deliberate, not decorative
- Contrast should be subtle, not loud

Purpose:
- Improve scannability
- Increase perceived depth
- Signal investment and scale

---

### Section Container Rules

Each major section should:
- Have clear top and bottom padding
- Feel visually bounded
- Align to a consistent max-width grid

No section should visually bleed into the next without separation.

---

### Card Usage (Encouraged)

Cards may be used for:
- Product blocks
- Feature summaries
- Structured informational content

Card Rules:
- Flat design (no heavy shadows)
- Subtle border or background separation
- Consistent padding and radius
- Cards must align in clean grids

Cards are for **structure**, not decoration.

---

### Home Page Framing Rules

The Home page must:
- Clearly separate hero, product, and informational sections
- Never feel like a single scrolling column
- Use framing to create a modular, expandable feel

Each section should look like it could stand alone.

---

### Product Page Framing Rules

Product pages must:
- Visually separate:
  - Overview
  - Specifications (structure only)
  - Documents / inquiry actions
- Use framing to reinforce technical seriousness

Avoid casual or blog-like layouts.

---

### About Page Framing Rules

About page must:
- Separate company overview, founder message, and supporting content
- Use framing to elevate credibility, not storytelling
- Founder section should feel formal and intentional

---

### Forbidden Techniques

- Decorative shapes
- Random lines or dividers
- Over-stylized section edges
- Section-level animations

---

### Success Criteria

At a glance, pages should feel:
- Structured
- Deliberate
- Heavy enough to signal scale
- Calm, not busy

---

### Guardrails
- Framing must support content, not dominate it
- No visual gimmicks
- Consistency across all pages is mandatory

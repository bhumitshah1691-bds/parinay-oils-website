Industrial Manufacturing

Castor oil is used as a raw material and functional input in multiple industrial formulations. Its viscosity and chemical stability support consistency in manufacturing processes.

Pharmaceuticals

In pharmaceutical applications, castor oil is used as an excipient and carrier. It supports formulation stability and controlled delivery in specific medicinal products.

Cosmetics & Personal Care

Castor oil is used for its emollient properties and texture-enhancing role. It contributes to moisture retention and formulation smoothness in cosmetic products.

Lubricants

Castor oil is applied in specialty lubricants where high-temperature performance and film strength are required. Its natural lubricity supports friction reduction.

Paints & Coatings

In paints and coatings, castor oil derivatives are used to influence flow, binding, and finish characteristics. It supports uniform application and durability.

Chemicals

Castor oil acts as a base input for various chemical derivatives. These derivatives are used across industrial and specialty chemical applications.

Agriculture Processing

Castor-based inputs are used in select agricultural processing applications. They support formulation requirements where plant-derived inputs are preferred.

Ayurveda

In Ayurvedic applications, castor oil is traditionally used as a base oil. It is processed and applied according to formulation-specific practices and intended therapeutic use.

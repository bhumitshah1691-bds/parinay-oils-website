# Phase 9.5 — Content & Asset Freeze
## 03_SPECS_AND_TECHNICALS

This file defines how technical specifications are presented on the website.

The website does NOT publish numeric or guaranteed specifications.
Specifications are shared only through inquiry-based documentation.

---

## Specification Philosophy

Technical details vary based on:
- Buyer requirements
- Application context
- Contractual agreement

The website presents specification structure only, not confirmed values.

---

## Castor Oil — Specification Structure

The following parameters are commonly referenced during buyer evaluation:

- Appearance
- Odour
- Density
- Viscosity
- Acid Value
- Moisture Content
- Purity Level

Numeric values for these parameters are:
- Buyer-specific
- Application-dependent
- Shared through official documents upon inquiry

---

## Castor Seed — Specification Structure

The following parameters are typically referenced for sourcing and supply:

- Seed Grade
- Moisture Content
- Foreign Matter
- Oil Content
- Packaging Format

All parameters are:
- Subject to sourcing conditions
- Confirmed at the time of supply

---

## Website Presentation Rule

On all product pages, include the statement:

“Detailed specifications and technical data are available on request.”

---

## Document Reference Rule

Generic technical overview documents may be linked for buyer orientation.
These documents are:
- Indicative only
- Non-binding
- Not certificates or guarantees

---

## Forbidden Actions

- No numeric specification values
- No implied standards (ASTM, ISO, BIS, etc.)
- No downloadable COAs
- No certification references unless confirmed separately

---

## Change Control

If verified specification sheets or certificates are provided by the client:
- This file must be updated first
- Only then may numeric specifications appear anywhere on the website

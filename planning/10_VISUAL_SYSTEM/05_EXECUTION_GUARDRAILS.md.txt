# Phase 10 — Visual & UX System
## 05_EXECUTION_GUARDRAILS

### Explicitly Forbidden
- Animations and motion effects
- Carousels and sliders
- Gradient-heavy designs
- Decorative icons without meaning

### Styling Limits
- No visual elements that imply certification or scale
- No visual claims stronger than textual claims

### Content Protection
- Text content is locked from Phase 7
- No rewriting for design reasons

### Change Control
- Any visual change must respect:
  - Manufacturing vs Traded separation
  - Compliance tone
  - Export-grade seriousness

### Cursor Discipline
- One visual change per prompt
- No “improve UI” instructions
- Every execution step must reference Phase 10 files

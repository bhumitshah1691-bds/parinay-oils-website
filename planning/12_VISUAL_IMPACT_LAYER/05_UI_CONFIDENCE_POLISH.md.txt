# Phase 12 — Visual Impact Layer
## 05_UI_CONFIDENCE_POLISH

### Objective
Introduce subtle UI cues that signal completion, confidence, and professionalism without appearing flashy or decorative.

These elements should be felt more than noticed.

---

### Button System

Primary Buttons:
- Solid brand green
- Clear rectangular shape
- Confident size (not small, not oversized)
- Consistent usage across site

Rules:
- One primary button per section
- No gradient or glossy effects
- No animated icons inside buttons

---

### Secondary Actions

Secondary actions (e.g. document links):
- Text links or subtle outlined buttons
- Clearly labeled as secondary
- Never visually compete with primary CTAs

---

### Hover & Interaction Feedback

Allowed:
- Subtle color darkening/lightening on hover
- Minimal transition timing

Forbidden:
- Bounce effects
- Scale animations
- Glow or shadow pop-ups

Interaction should feel calm and deliberate.

---

### Dividers & Separators

Allowed:
- Thin lines
- Spacing-based separation
- Subtle background changes

Forbidden:
- Decorative separators
- Icons used as dividers
- Heavy visual breaks

---

### Typography Finishing

- Headings must maintain consistent spacing
- Avoid overly tight line spacing
- Maintain hierarchy clarity across all pages

No typography experimentation allowed at this stage.

---

### Consistency Rule

The same UI behaviors must apply:
- Across all pages
- Across all sections
- Across all device widths (desktop-first)

No one-off styling.

---

### Success Criteria

The site should feel:
- Calm
- Finished
- Confident
- Serious

Visitors should sense effort and investment without distraction.

---

### Guardrails

- No animations added outside defined hover behavior
- No stylistic elements that conflict with Phase 12 goals
- UI polish must support content, not overpower it

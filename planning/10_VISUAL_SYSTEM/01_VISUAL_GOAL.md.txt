# Phase 10 — Visual & UX System
## 01_VISUAL_GOAL

### Target Perception
- Export-grade
- Trust-first
- Industrial premium
- Calm, competent, established

### Primary Audience
- B2B buyers
- Procurement teams
- Overseas distributors and partners

### Emotional Outcome
The site should immediately communicate operational seriousness and long-term reliability without trying to impress.

### Must Feel Like
- Manufacturing-led credibility
- Process-driven organization
- Established exporter, not an experiment

### Must NOT Feel Like
- Startup or SaaS
- FMCG or D2C brand
- Flashy, animated, or promotional
- Template-driven or theme-based

### Reference Types (not brands)
- Industrial exporters
- Chemical and commodity manufacturers
- Infrastructure and materials suppliers

### Success Test
If all visuals were removed, the site should still feel complete and credible. Visuals must only reinforce trust, never compensate for missing substance.

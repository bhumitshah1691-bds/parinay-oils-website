# Phase 12 — Visual Impact Layer
## 02_HERO_SECTION_SYSTEM

### Objective
Create strong first-glance authority aligned with Indian business psychology:
- Visible investment
- Sense of scale
- Site must feel alive, not static

The hero section must impress immediately without appearing gimmicky or promotional.

---

### Hero Placement
- Home page only
- First visible section
- Full-width (edge-to-edge background)
- Large vertical height (minimum 75vh desktop)

---

### Core Hero Philosophy
Indian buyers equate:
- Movement = effort
- Scale = credibility
- Visual weight = seriousness

The hero must balance show-off with control.

---

### Allowed Motion (STRICT)
Only ONE motion type is allowed:

✔ Slow scroll-based parallax on background image
- Image moves slower than page scroll
- Motion must be subtle and continuous
- No zoom
- No bounce
- No easing theatrics

Purpose:
- Make the page feel alive
- Signal investment and modernity
- Add depth without distraction

---

### Forbidden Motion (ABSOLUTE)
- No text animation
- No fade-ins
- No sliding elements
- No auto-playing motion
- No carousels
- No video backgrounds

Foreground content must remain rock-solid and stable.

---

### Content Structure (LOCKED)
1. Primary headline (largest text on site)
2. Supporting statement (short, factual)
3. One primary call-to-action
4. Supporting visual (background image)

No additional elements allowed.

---

### Headline Rules
- Direct
- Declarative
- Industrial, not marketing

Good:
“Manufacturers and Suppliers of Castor Oil and Castor Seed”

Bad:
“Delivering Excellence Worldwide”

---

### Visual Treatment
- Background image preferred (real, industrial, product or commodity related)
- If image is unavailable:
  - Use brand green background
  - Use layout dominance instead of imagery

---

### Optional Enhancement (ALLOWED)
✔ Subtle dark overlay or gradient overlay
- Used only to improve text contrast
- Must be barely noticeable
- No decorative gradients
- No color effects

---

### Call-to-Action Rules
- One primary button only
- Solid brand green
- Confident size
- Neutral wording (Contact Us / Send Inquiry)

---

### Psychological Target
Viewer reaction within 3 seconds:
“Big company. Serious agro business. This is not a small setup.”

---

### Guardrails
- No additional animation types without Phase 12 revision
- Motion must never compete with content
- Hero sets tone; rest of site stays calmer

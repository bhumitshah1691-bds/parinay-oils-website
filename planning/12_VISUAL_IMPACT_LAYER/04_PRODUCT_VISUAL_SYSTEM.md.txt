# Phase 12 — Visual Impact Layer
## 04_PRODUCT_VISUAL_SYSTEM

### Objective
Make products feel credible, industrial, and commercially serious through visual hierarchy, sizing, and structure.

The goal is to avoid “catalog text” perception and instead signal real commodity business presence.

---

### Product Visual Philosophy

Indian B2B buyers judge product credibility by:
- Image size
- Clarity of separation
- Technical presentation

Products must feel:
- Large enough to matter
- Cleanly presented
- Free from marketing exaggeration

---

### Product Image Usage Rules

Allowed:
- One primary image per product
- Image must be visually dominant within the product section
- Image must appear before or alongside key product information

Rules:
- No image galleries
- No sliders
- No multiple angles
- No decorative overlays

Images support credibility, not aesthetics.

---

### Image Sizing & Placement

- Product images must be large enough to anchor the section
- Images should not feel like thumbnails
- Maintain generous spacing around images

Preferred layouts:
- Image on one side, content on the other
- Image aligned to section grid, not floating

---

### Product Content Grouping

Each product section must be visually divided into:

1. Product Overview  
2. Specification Structure (non-numeric)  
3. Documents / Inquiry Actions  

Each group must be clearly framed and separated.

---

### Product Differentiation Rules

- Manufactured products (Castor Oil) must feel more controlled and technical
- Traded products (Castor Seed) must feel clearly labeled as sourced

Visual hierarchy must reflect this distinction subtly, without text repetition.

---

### Document Linking Rules

- Technical overview PDFs must appear as secondary actions
- Links must be clearly labeled as “Indicative” or “Technical Overview”
- No visual emphasis suggesting certificates or guarantees

---

### Forbidden Techniques

- No lifestyle imagery
- No consumer-style product shots
- No glossy FMCG presentation
- No certification-style icons near products

---

### Success Criteria

Product pages should feel:
- Technical
- Serious
- Commercially oriented
- Suitable for inquiry-driven sales

---

### Guardrails

- No visual claims stronger than text claims
- Product visuals must align with Phase 9.5 content freeze
- Consistency across all product pages is mandatory

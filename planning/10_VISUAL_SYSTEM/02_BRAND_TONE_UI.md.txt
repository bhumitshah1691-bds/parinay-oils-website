# Phase 10 — Visual & UX System
## 02_BRAND_TONE_UI

### Brand Posture
- Serious
- Professional
- Export-oriented
- Compliance-aware

### UI Personality
- Quiet confidence
- Minimal but not empty
- Structured, not decorative

### Formality Level
- Formal language
- No conversational tone
- No storytelling flourishes

### Density Preference
- Medium–low density
- Clear separation between sections
- Comfortable reading without scrolling fatigue

### Modern vs Conservative
- Visually modern
- Behaviorally conservative
- No experimental patterns

### B2B Bias
- Designed for evaluation, not persuasion
- Supports scanning, verification, and comparison

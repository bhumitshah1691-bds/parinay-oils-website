Section Type: Grid-based, hover-dominant interaction

Default State:
- All application cards visible
- Clean, neutral appearance
- No motion until user interacts

Hover Behavior:
- Card lifts slightly on hover
- Background darkens or accent border appears
- Icon or image subtly scales
- Text clarity increases

Scroll Behavior:
- Cards enter with staggered fade + upward motion
- No scroll hijacking
- Motion completes before hover starts

User Feedback:
- Cursor movement causes immediate reaction
- Clear sense of interactivity
- No delayed or hidden effects

Text Priority:
- Title visible at all times
- Description readable without hover
- Hover enhances, does not reveal hidden content

Performance Notes:
- CSS + lightweight motion preferred
- Avoid heavy JS for hover effects

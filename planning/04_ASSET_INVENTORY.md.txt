SECTION B — ASSET GAP ANALYSIS

CRITICAL ASSETS (REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH)
- Company logo (clean, usable version)
- Accurate company address and contact details
- Product understanding visuals (diagrams acceptable)
- Manufacturing process flow diagram (abstract, not photographic)

OPTIONAL ASSETS (POST-LAUNCH IMPROVEMENT)

- Leadership photography

DO NOT CREATE / DO NOT FAKE
- Certification visuals without actual certification
- Stock factory imagery implying ownership
- Sustainability claims without operational proof
- Client logos or testimonials

PROJECT: Parinay Oils – Corporate Website
PHASE: 0 — Strategic Intent & Constraints (FROZEN)

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
- Establish a credible digital presence for Parinay Oils
- Position the company as a serious, export-ready manufacturer and trader of castor oil
- Website is informational and brand-driven, not conversion-driven

REFERENCE CONTEXT (COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE)
The website must be inspired by the structural depth, seriousness, and restraint of:
- https://www.gokulagro.com/
- https://www.ambujasolvex.com/
- https://gokuloverseas.com/
- https://modioilmill.com/
- https://www.jayantagro.com/
- https://girnarindustries.com/

Inspiration is allowed for:
- Information hierarchy
- Section depth
- Tone and restraint
- Trust-building patterns

Copying is NOT allowed for:
- Visual design
- Text
- Claims
- Layouts

TARGET AUDIENCE
- B2B buyers (domestic and international)
- Importers / exporters
- Distributors
- Industry partners
- Regulatory or compliance reviewers (secondary)

POSITIONING PRINCIPLES
- Manufacturing-first mindset
- Trading as a capability, not the core identity
- Ahmedabad, Gujarat as an industrial strength signal
- Conservative, export-grade communication
- Confidence through facts, not adjectives

SCOPE (HARD LIMITS)
- Static informational website
- Maximum 25 pages total
- Desktop-first, mobile responsive
- English language only (unless explicitly expanded later)

EXCLUSIONS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- Ecommerce or pricing
- User accounts or dashboards
- Lead funnels or aggressive CTAs
- Blog growth strategy
- Marketing gimmicks
- Claims that cannot be verified with proof

CONTENT PHILOSOPHY
- Inform, not persuade
- Explain, not sell
- Show capability, not ambition
- Clarity over creativity
- Substance over style

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
- Industrial and minimal
- Low visual noise
- Real assets over stock imagery
- Structure before aesthetics
- Function over flair

TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS
- No CMS unless strictly necessary
- SEO fundamentals only (no content marketing)
- No heavy animations or performance-heavy effects

DECISION RULE
When in doubt, ask:
“Would a serious international buyer trust this?”

If the answer is unclear, remove or simplify.

CHANGE CONTROL
- Any deviation from this document resets planning to Phase 0
- This file is frozen after approval and must not be edited
